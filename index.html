Privacy policy

Your financial data stays yours.

Last updated: 13 June 2026. Vaulx is built as a local-first expense tracker: no ads, no analytics SDKs, no automatic cloud upload and no account required.

Local storage

Expenses, categories, budgets, recurring expenses, levels, quests and app settings are saved on the user's device.

No tracking or ads

Vaulx does not use analytics SDKs, does not profile users and does not show third-party ads.

Encrypted protection

The local database is protected with SQLCipher encryption. Sensitive settings use encrypted Android storage where supported.

Manual backup

Backups are optional and start only when requested by the user. Backup files are encrypted before sharing or saving.

No internet required

Vaulx does not require internet to record, view, back up or restore expenses.

User control

The user can export or delete data from app settings. Contact: vaulxapp@gmail.com.
