Privacy policy

Financial data stays yours.

Last updated: 21 June 2026. Vaulx is designed as a local-first application. Core features work without an account, advertising profile or analytics SDK.

Contact: vaulxapp@gmail.com

Local encrypted storage

Expenses, categories, budgets, recurring expenses, loyalty cards, levels and quests are stored on the device in an SQLCipher-protected database or encrypted Android storage.

No analytics or advertising

Vaulx does not include advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics or third-party profiling tools.

Camera processing

The camera is used only when the user chooses to scan a barcode or QR code. Recognition happens on-device and captured frames are not stored or transmitted by Vaulx.

Manual encrypted backup

A backup is created only on request, encrypted using the recovery password and shared only through the destination selected by the user.

No internet required for core use

Tracking expenses, viewing statistics, managing budgets, loyalty cards, backups and restores does not require a Vaulx account.

User control

Users can modify or delete their records and erase all local app data from Settings at any time.
